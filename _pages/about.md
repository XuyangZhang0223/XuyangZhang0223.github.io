Hi! I am a final-year Ph.D. candidate in the [Robot Perception Lab (RPL)](https://kings-rpl.github.io/) at [King’s College London](https://www.kcl.ac.uk/), supervised by Prof. [Shan Luo](https://shanluo.github.io/). I received my Master’s degree in Robotics from the [University of Bristol](https://www.bristol.ac.uk/) in 2022, where I was supervised by Prof. [Nathan Lepora](https://lepora.com/) and Dr. [Dandan Zhang](https://www.intelligentrobotics-acrossscales.com/). During my time there, I worked on the simulation, fabrication, and validation of tactile-based soft grippers and their applications in robotics.

My research lies at the intersection of `multimodal Physical AI`, `tactile sensing`, and `dexterous robot manipulation`, with the broader goal of enabling robots to perceive and interact robustly with the physical world through `vision and touch`. A central theme of my Ph.D. research is to endow robots with new forms of dexterity by actively redesigning the contact interface, particularly through `rotatable tactile fingertips` that combine sensing and actuation to enable continuous and controllable contact with the environment. Building on this idea, my work spans bio-inspired and embodied tactile sensing, active tactile manipulation, physics-based tactile simulation and Sim2Real transfer, and predictive multimodal learning for contact-rich manipulation. More broadly, I am interested in the integration of `robot hand design, perception, and learning-based control` to develop robotic systems that can exploit rich physical interaction for robust, adaptive, and generalizable dexterity.

Outside of research, I enjoy playing badminton, drawing, capturing beautiful moments through photography, and running along the River Thames.
